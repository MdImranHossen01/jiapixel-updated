## 🤖 AI Video Generator (Veo 3.1) Text Prompts

যদি আপনি পুরো ১০ সেকেন্ডের ভিডিও একবারে অথবা পার্ট-বাই-পার্ট জেনারেট করতে চান, তবে নিচের প্রম্পটগুলো ব্যবহার করতে পারেন:

### Prompt Option 1: Full 10-Second Sequence (Single Shot/Continuous Motion)
> **Prompt:** A 10-second commercial video for a premium e-commerce website builder. Starts with a close-up of a high-tech smartphone floating in a dark background with neon purple and teal light accents. The screen shows a beautiful glassmorphic online store UI. Camera smoothly pans back as shiny 3D icons of a delivery truck, a gold security lock, and a cute glowing robot head orbit around the phone. The phone then gently slides to the side to reveal a matching sleek laptop displaying a modern web page. Smooth transitions, dynamic camera movement, product advertisement style, photorealistic, 8k resolution, Unreal Engine 5 render style, high-end commercial look.

### Prompt Option 2: Split Scenes (For better control during editing)
*   **Scene 1 (0-3s):** `A close-up shot of a modern smartphone displaying a colorful, glowing glassmorphic e-commerce store application. Neon purple and cyan light trails orbit the phone in a dark studio setting. Cinematic lighting, fast-paced rotation, 3D render style, 8k, premium commercial.`
*   **Scene 2 (3-7s):** `3D glossy icons of a delivery truck with a GPS radar, a gold padlock, and an AI chat symbol floating in mid-air with soft animations. Clean studio background with abstract light grids, premium tech-look, vibrant colors.`
*   **Scene 3 (7-10s):** `A modern workspace with a sleek laptop showing a beautiful e-commerce website landing page. A glowing holographic button with "Order Now" pops up on the side. Elegant particles in the air, high contrast, clean minimalist aesthetic.`

## 🎙️ Voiceover (VO) Guidance

*   **টোন (Tone):** এনার্জেটিক, আত্মবিশ্বাসী, প্রফেশনাল এবং দ্রুত (Energetic, Professional & Fast-paced).
*   **স্পিড (Speed):** যেহেতু ভিডিওটি ১০ সেকেন্ডের, তাই ভয়েসওভার ডেলিভারি বেশ শার্প ও দ্রুত হতে হবে (প্রায় ২৬-৩০টি শব্দ)।
